About me

Curious by default, building with intent

I got into AI the way most people do lately, by being endlessly curious about how machines seem to understand us. That curiosity turned into hands-on practice: cleaning messy datasets, training my first models, and slowly learning to tell the difference between a model that looks right and one that actually is.

These days I split my time between structured learning and shipping small, real projects, because a concept only really sticks once I've broken it and fixed it myself. I'm most interested in the practical middle ground between research and product: taking a model out of a notebook and into something people can actually use.
